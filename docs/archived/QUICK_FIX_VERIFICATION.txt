KEY POINTS:

🔑 P&L ONLY COUNTS DELIVERED ORDERS
   └─ draft, confirmed, processing, packed, shipped, out_for_delivery = ₹0
   └─ delivered = ✅ COUNTS
   └─ This is why orders weren't showing in P&L before

🔑 ALL BUTTONS ARE SEQUENTIAL
   └─ Can't skip steps (backend blocks invalid transitions)
   └─ Must go in order: processing → packed → shipped → delivered

🔑 TRACKING NUMBER IS OPTIONAL
   └─ When you click "🚚 Mark Shipped", tracking prompt appears
   └─ Can leave blank or paste number (India Post, Delhivery, etc.)

🔑 STATUS HISTORY LOGGED
   └─ Each status change is recorded with timestamp
   └─ See history at bottom of order drawer
